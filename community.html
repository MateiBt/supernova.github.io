Supernova Guide is officially live.

We built Supernova Guide to put structured Olympiad theory handouts, past USAAAO problem sets, a 3D planetarium, and regular seasonal competitions in one place. Registration for the Supernova Astrophysics Cup (Autumn 2026) and our first cycle of daily, weekly, and monthly problems are now open.
