Official Format & Rules
  • Qualification Cutoffs: Top 30 advance from Phase 1 (Open Qualifier) to Phase 2 (Selection Round). Top 20 advance from Phase 2 to Phase 3 (Grand Final).
  • Scoring & Tie-Breakers: \(+1.0\text{ pt}\) per correct answer, \(0.0\text{ pts}\) for blank or wrong choices (no negative marking). Ties are broken by the average difficulty weight of solved problems.
  • Mock Exam & Proctoring: Official Mock Exam runs Thursday, 19 Nov 2026 (14:00–20:00 UT) with a 60-minute timer, dual-camera + screen proctoring, and a strict 1-attempt limit per Bib ID and email.
  • Candidate Bib Code Anonymity: Public rosters and scoreboards display only your official Candidate Bib Code (SNG-2026-10XX) to protect student privacy.
