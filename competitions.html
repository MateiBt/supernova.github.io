Official Format & Rules
  • Eligibility & Teams: Open worldwide to students up to age 20. Compete Individually or in a Team of 2 (with a chosen partner or randomly assigned). Teams of 2 receive a Special Team Diploma, and each participant must submit the registration form.
  • Scoring & Tie-Breakers: \(+1.0\text{ pt}\) per correct answer, \(0.0\text{ pts}\) for blank or wrong choices (no negative marking). Ties are broken by the average difficulty weight of solved problems.
  • Universal Entry Window: All rounds open between 18:00 UT and 19:00 UT on their respective dates.
  • Allowed Tools & Proctoring: Non-programmable scientific calculator and the official IAU Constants sheet (`tools.html`). Full-screen lockdown and webcam proctoring apply.
