SAC 2026 Format & Scoring Archive
  • Grand Final Schedule (Completed): Held on Saturday, 21 Nov 2026 across two international time zones — Window A (10:00–11:00 UT) and Window B (18:00–19:00 UT) with a 90-minute exam timer.
  • Scoring & Tie-Breakers: \(+1.0\text{ pt}\) per correct answer across 45 MCQs (10 Easy, 20 Medium, 15 Hard), \(0.0\text{ pts}\) for blank or wrong choices (no negative marking). Ties were resolved by the average difficulty weight (\(\text{TB}\)) of solved problems.
  • Integrity & Validation: All Grand Final scripts were independently audited by the Scientific Committee prior to publishing the official 15-entry standings.
  • Unified Ranking Field: Solo contestants and 2-person teams competed together on a unified global leaderboard identified publicly by Candidate Bib Code & Callsign.
