    3D Sky Simulator & Observational Training

    Practice for observational rounds or learn the night sky on a 3D celestial sphere built from real star and deep-sky catalogs. Identify navigation stars, estimate apparent magnitudes, and find Messier and Caldwell objects without coordinate grids.

    Controls:

    • Pan: Left-click and drag to rotate the sky.
    • Zoom: Scroll wheel to change your Field of View (FOV) and inspect dense star fields.
    • Select: Click directly on any star or Deep Sky Object to lock onto it.

    Modes:

    • Singleplayer: Timed 5, 15, or 30-minute runs (or unlimited Free Roam) across Name Identification, Object Classification, Target Pinpointing, and Magnitude Estimation.
    • Arena (1v1 & Ranked ELO): Play locally, against AI bots, or in live online rooms.
    • Tournaments: Elimination survival, knockout brackets, or the 36-player Champions Cup.
    • Constellations: Speed-run individual IAU constellations to earn Bronze, Silver, and Gold medals.
