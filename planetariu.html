    Welcome to Supernova Planetarium

    This module provides an interactive 3D simulation of the celestial sphere based on real astronomical data. It is designed to train and evaluate your observational skills across thousands of stars and Deep Sky Objects.

    Instructions & Controls:

    • Navigation: Use the mouse (Left Click & Drag) to pan across the celestial sphere.
    • Optical Zoom: Use the scroll wheel to zoom in and inspect dense constellations or deep sky regions.
    • Selection: Click on any target to lock onto it.

    Getting Started:

    • Go to Singleplayer to test your skills in specific gamemodes (Name, Type, Magnitude, etc.) with strict timers and leaderboards.
    • Go to Arena to challenge your friends, play against the A.I. Bot, or compete in Online Ranked matches for ELO.
    • Go to Constellations for an isolated practice session on specific star patterns.
