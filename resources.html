External Resources

A curated collection of the best books, databases, and software for Astronomy Olympiad preparation.

Textbooks & Guides

Theory & Fundamentals

  • An Introduction to Modern Astrophysics(Carroll & Ostlie)
    The "Big Orange Book" (BOB). The gold standard for IOAA.
  • Fundamental Astronomy(Karttunen et al.)
    Best for spherical astronomy and celestial mechanics basics.

Problem Solving

  • Schaum's Outlines - Astronomy
    Quick, practical problems for reinforcing concepts.
  • A Problem Book in Astronomy & Astrophysics(Aniket Sule)
    Official IOAA-style problems compilation.
