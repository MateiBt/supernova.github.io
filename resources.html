Core Textbooks

The standard books for Olympiad theory, spherical trigonometry, and astrophysics derivations.

An Introduction to Modern Astrophysics

Bradley W. Carroll & Dale A. Ostlie

The "Big Orange Book" (BOB). The go-to reference for binary star systems, stellar atmospheres and interiors, galactic dynamics, and cosmology.

Fundamental Astronomy

Hannu Karttunen et al.

Best concise reference for spherical trigonometry, coordinate transformations, magnitudes, radiation physics, and celestial mechanics.

Astronomy: Principles and Practice

A. E. Roy & D. Clarke

Essential for positional astronomy, timekeeping (sidereal/solar time, equation of time), atmospheric refraction, and telescope optics.

A Problem Book in Astronomy & Astrophysics

Aniket Sule

Compilation of past International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA) Theory, Data Analysis, and Observational problems.

Catalogs & Imaging Tools

Look up coordinates, spectral types, proper motions, redshifts, and photometry for stars, clusters, and galaxies.
