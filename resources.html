Textbooks & Literature

Core academic textbooks and problem compilations required for IOAA theoretical mastery.

An Introduction to Modern Astrophysics

Bradley W. Carroll & Dale A. Ostlie

The "Big Orange Book" (BOB). The definitive reference textbook for stellar structure, spectra, galaxies, and cosmology.

Fundamental Astronomy

Hannu Karttunen et al.

Essential coverage of spherical trigonometry, coordinate transformations, time systems, and celestial mechanics.

A Problem Book in Astronomy & Astrophysics

Aniket Sule

Official compilation of past International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA) problems and solutions.
